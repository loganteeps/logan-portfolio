I am a mechanical and aerospace engineering student at Olin College of Engineering. My focus is in space, aviation, and generally making things fly. I pursue this passion through my flying career, and at Olin, I am the mechanical and aerodynamics Lead for the Olin AERO sUAS Team. I'm also the lead mechanical engineer for a 175 kg brushfire attack drone. In addition to aviation, I am on the drivetrain sub-team of Olin Baja. Outside of engineering, my great passions are surfing (and building surfboards), sailing, skiing, and scuba diving. I enjoy sharing my passion for STEM through tutoring math and science to High School and Middle School students.
